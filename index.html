The Problem

Farmers Are Losing the Battle They Shouldn't

India's agriculture sector is plagued by systemic inefficiencies that drain the income and trust of millions of farmers every season.

💸

High Input Costs

Farmers pay 30–60% more for seeds, fertilizers, and pesticides due to multi-layer middlemen with no price visibility or competitive benchmarking.

⚠️

Fake & Substandard Products

Counterfeit agri-inputs flood rural markets, causing crop failures, financial ruin, and deepening distrust toward modern farming solutions.

🌫️

Zero Transparency

No standard pricing, no seller accountability, no access to agronomic knowledge — farmers navigate a broken market blindly, every single season.
